Fake anaglyph stereo from depth in realtime compositor????!
[protected]#

[protected] [protected] [protected] stereoscopy in viewport cam via real-time compositor? Oh wait but that still requires cams. Aha, realtime compositor can use viewport camera. And, maybe I can fake stereoscopic vision from a monocular camera's depth map? Yes you can! And in fact, this is even better than “real” stereo b/c the rendering cost hasn't doubled.

Although, there is slight artifacting b/c you can't see behind things in a depth map so you have to assume it's filled in or a  hole. As such, thin objects look especially bad. Also, no transparency. Actually, GPT just told me one solution could be to render multiple depth layers, smart — but this means you can't use occlusion, and in some cases rm'ing occlusion could even make the render slower than just having 2 cameras. Well, what if I slightly shrink/anti-dilate the objects before making the depth map? That way I could “see” around edges a little. Or, even better maybe, I could use a similar strat to the eevee fisheye effect I saw a couple days ago. Basically distort the geometry to widen it the farther away it is to get fake info about being able to look around the back of things. However, this would require basically rendering twice too, so, maybe not any faster. And in fact, apparently a true stereo image is not actually x2 the cost: “Most of the expensive work—building acceleration structures, loading geometry, compiling shaders—has already been done. The second render often costs much less than twice as much, especially if you're already path tracing.”
And the best part of all of this, is I can still drive the compositor node algo's fake stereo “convergence plane” using the 3D cursor position, by using a driver in the compositor nodes, I think.

Whoa, and ano/ great idea from GPT: I can use more than only depth info when creating the anaglyph img. I can include curvature information to make the peaks and valleys “pop” more.
Here's a blog article about a guy who made stereo image from depthmap, w/ links to resources/scripts that might still be releveant, like a GIMP script I could adapt.

This could even be an add-on! [protected] What a fun, fun idea.

More reasons why a compositor-based anaglyph stereo is good: I can edit the anaglph colors on the fly as my eyes are adjusting to keep ghosting minimal, instead of being stuck w/ Blender's pre-set red/cyan | green/magenta | blue/yellow options. In addition, it makes it s.t. even if ppl got a bad/non-standard pair of 3D glasses, they can edit the colors to whatever work best for them to reduce ghosting.
